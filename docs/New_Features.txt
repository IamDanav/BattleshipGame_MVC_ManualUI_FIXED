NEW FEATURES

1. Manual Ship Placement: the player places ships by clicking cells.
2. Orientation Button: horizontal or vertical ship placement.
3. Start Battle Button: battle starts after all ships are placed.
4. Battleship Style UI: light blue water board with dot markers.
5. MVC Improvement: Model, View, and Controller are separated.
6. Computer AI: after a hit, the computer tries nearby cells first.
